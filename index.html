Common questions

Why does a scan pause?

Scanning pauses under Low Power Mode or elevated thermal pressure to protect battery life and performance. Completed work is saved; tap Resume Scan later.

Does the app delete automatically?

No. You select items, request deletion, and approve Apple Photos’ system confirmation. Deleted items follow the Photos app’s Recently Deleted behavior.

What does Keep do?

Keep removes selected photos or videos from cleanup candidates and prevents them from being considered in later scans. You can clear kept-item decisions in Settings.

Why are some items unavailable?

Cloud-only media is skipped unless “Download needed iCloud thumbnails” is enabled. Limited Photos access also restricts the scan to the items you selected.

How does the free version work?

The full visible library is analyzed and included in statistics. The free version reveals a varied preview of up to 10 detected items; Premium reveals all candidates.

How do I manage Premium?

Use Restore Purchases in Settings if access is missing. Subscriptions can be viewed or canceled from your Apple Account’s subscription settings.
