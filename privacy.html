KidCam Cleaner
Support Privacy

Effective August 13, 2026

Privacy Policy

KidCam Cleaner is designed so that your photographs and analysis stay on your Apple device.

Who operates the app

KidCam Cleaner is published by Kuanysh Italmassov. Questions about this policy can be sent to italmas2010@gmail.com.

Data collection

KidCam Cleaner does not collect personal data through the app, create user accounts, track users, show advertising, include analytics SDKs, or transmit photographs or analysis results to the developer or a third party.

Photo Library access

Photo access is used only to provide features you request. Analysis uses Apple Photos, Core Graphics, and Vision technologies on the device. The app does not upload photos or maintain a separate copy of the original image.

If you enable iCloud downloads, Apple Photos may download a photo from your own iCloud Photo Library to the device. KidCam Cleaner does not receive or upload that photo.

Information stored on your device

The app stores derived analysis metadata locally, which may include image-quality measurements, perceptual hashes, Apple Vision feature prints, Photos local identifiers, grouping identifiers, scan progress, kept-photo decisions, and detection preferences. This information is used only to avoid unnecessary rescanning and provide cleanup features.

Deletion

KidCam Cleaner never silently deletes photos. You select candidates and initiate a PhotoKit change that is subject to Apple’s system confirmation. Deleted items follow the Photos app’s normal Recently Deleted behavior.

Purchases

Premium subscriptions are processed by Apple through StoreKit. The developer does not receive your full payment details. Apple’s privacy practices apply to App Store transactions.

Retention and your choices

Derived analysis metadata remains on your device until you clear cached analysis or delete the app. You can clear kept-photo decisions and reset detection learning in Settings. Photo access can be changed at any time in iOS Settings.

Support communications

If you email support, the developer receives the information you choose to include and uses it only to respond to your request and resolve the issue. Avoid sending private photographs unless necessary. Support correspondence is deleted when it is no longer needed for those purposes.

Children

The app is a utility for photo-library owners. It is not directed to children, does not intentionally collect information from children, and does not attempt to identify who captured a photo.

This website

This support site does not use cookies or developer-operated analytics. It is hosted by GitHub Pages, so GitHub may process limited technical information needed to deliver the site under the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

Policy updates

This policy may be updated if the app’s features or legal requirements change. The effective date at the top of this page will identify the latest version.
